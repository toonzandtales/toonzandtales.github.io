Meet the characters

The friends who sing, wobble and help in our songs and musicals.

Wobble Town Rescue Crew cast

Fizzle

Fizzle is a small round penguin with a soft blue-grey back and cream belly, a teal knitted beanie with a white pom-pom and a soft orange and cream striped scarf. The wobbly leader of the crew, who always waddles in to help.

Duckie is a cheerful yellow duckling with a small orange beak. Duckie paddles, flaps and zooms ahead to help first.

Tilly the Tugboat

Tilly the Tugboat is a small chubby orange-and-teal wooden tugboat with big friendly eyes, a pink flower on her bow and a short striped yellow funnel. A guest rescuer who sails to every water emergency with a deep, friendly toot.
