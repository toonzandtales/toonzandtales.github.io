About Toonz and Tales

Original sing-along songs and story-song musicals for little ones.

Welcome to Toonz and Tales

Toonz and Tales is a home for original kids songs, nursery-style sing-alongs and story-songs for toddlers and preschoolers. Our characters are friendly animals and funny little helpers who sing about everyday adventures: counting, colours, kindness, sharing, bedtime, rainy days, helping friends and trying again.

Each song has simple words, a steady beat, handclaps and a chorus that little voices can shout along with, so it works for dancing in the living room, car rides and quiet singing together.

What you will find here

  • Full songs: animated sing-along videos. See all songs.
  • Shorts: quick sing-along clips of the best choruses. See all Shorts.
  • Musicals: story-songs told in weekly episodes, with a page for each musical: Wobble Town Rescue Crew.

Good to know for parents and carers

  • Songs are gentle and upbeat, with no scary parts, and this site has no ads or shopping.
  • The songs, characters and animation are made with AI tools under human direction, and the videos are labelled that way on YouTube.
  • This website has no sign-ups, comments or tracking. Videos open on YouTube.

Questions and answers

What is Toonz and Tales?

Toonz and Tales makes original kids songs and story-songs for toddlers and preschoolers, with friendly characters, simple words and big shout-along choruses. Everything is free to watch on YouTube.

What ages are the songs for?

The songs are made for little ones from about one to five years old, and they work well for families singing together. They are gentle, upbeat and easy to join in with.

What are the musicals?

Musicals are series of story-songs told in weekly episodes, with the same characters going on a new adventure each time. They are best watched in order, and each musical has its own page on this site.

How often are new videos released?

New songs and musical episodes are released regularly, and each musical page shows its release schedule. Subscribe on YouTube to be there when a new one arrives.

Where can I watch?

On the Toonz and Tales YouTube channel, or from the video pages on this site. Every picture on this site links to the video on YouTube.

How are the songs and videos made?

The songs, characters and animation are created with AI tools under human direction, and the videos are labelled as AI-made on YouTube.

Is this site safe for children?

This site has no ads, no sign-ups, no comments and no tracking. Videos open on YouTube, which has its own rules for made-for-kids content.
